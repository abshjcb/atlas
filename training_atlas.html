Scoring assumptions

Fractional-set accounting is a practical approximation. Direct/secondary/indirect coefficients are not universally validated conversions. Scores are analytical heuristics, not biological predictions.

Data management

Data is stored in this browser's localStorage. It is not automatically synced across devices or browsers. Export a JSON backup periodically. Private browsing, browser storage cleanup, or deleting site data can remove records.

Custom exercises

Map an exercise that isn't in the library to its muscles so it counts toward muscle estimates and the map. Optionally borrow another lift's strength standards to get a level.

Methodology & limitations

Effective sets: Direct contribution × 1.0, secondary × configurable coefficient, indirect × configurable coefficient. A drop-set marker does not add a full working set. Only checked actual exercises contribute to actual calculations.

Effort: Recorded RIR informs interpretation. Missing RIR is unknown, not assumed. ROM completeness and progression are displayed as context; they are not precise biological multipliers.

Volume: Reference bands are configurable heuristics. More sets do not automatically mean a higher score. Excessive concentration or volume can reduce the score.

Running: Conditioning is tracked separately. It does not add resistance sets or directly increase hypertrophy scores. Running involvement is qualitative and varies by pace, incline, mechanics and individual technique.

Growth: Workout logs can show performance and estimated training inputs. They cannot establish actual muscle growth without direct measurements and controlled conditions.
